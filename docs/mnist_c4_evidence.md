# Experimental provenance: trained MNIST C4 evidence

This page documents the **measured trained outcomes** presented in the repository homepage. They are separate from the analytic/synthetic illustrations generated by scripts/make_figures.py and separate from the simplified training preset in scripts/reproduce.py.

## Where the evidence comes from

These figures were constructed from the author's uploaded archival reports, not from invented synthetic positions:

- Original C4 study: `thesis_c4_20261101_162610_497375.zip`, SHA-256 `d5833e9495bbf28fefe72d88d576e3e0ff37d0d4eb71ead6dc77360bfef1211b`.
- Replication/robustness: `ROBUSTESSE_C4.zip`, SHA-256 `6231c6c857369b7a9dc66c13444e5b0db90f33b75ac35e808179a651bea64643`.

These are author-supplied research artifacts; the public repository contains only small, non-reversible **visual summaries**, not datasets, model weights, or the archives themselves.

**Protocol reported in the two archives:** MNIST digits 1, 2, 3, 4; 3-dimensional learned embeddings; **10 epochs**, minibatch 32, Adam learning rate 0.001, MES weight `beta=1.0` in the APML+MES arm; seeds **42/43/44**; full official MNIST training and test class subsets. The test split is used for descriptive evaluation after training; it does **not** define the trainable class proxies. These settings were documented as a **new controlled thesis-informed experiment**, not an exact reconstruction of the historic thesis's 3D figure or the journal paper's benchmark recipes.

## Figure 6: actual learned trainable class proxies

[Open figure 6](../figures/06_mnist_trained_proxies.svg).

- Source: `runs/apml/embeddings.npz` and `runs/apml_mes/embeddings.npz` in the original C4 archive, seed 42.
- Only the **four class-proxy vectors** from each trained checkpoint are drawn; dots representing test embeddings are **not shown in this concise figure**.
- All four proxies are real trained vectors in `R^3`; their 2D positions are an **orthographic display projection** (azimuth -59°, elevation +19°), not a fitted 2D model.
- Crucially, the six numbers accompanying the figure are the **actual Euclidean 3D distances** between proxy vectors, not projected distances. The projected 2D edge lengths are not all equal even for a true 3D regular tetrahedron.

| 3D proxy-pair distance | APML | APML + MES |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–2 | 8.3980 | 8.0962 |
| 1–3 | 11.1179 | 8.0962 |
| 1–4 | 7.3566 | 8.0962 |
| 2–3 | 6.9913 | 8.0961 |
| 2–4 | 11.1339 | 8.0960 |
| 3–4 | 8.6156 | 8.0960 |

Min/mean of the **3D Euclidean** pair distances is 0.782409 (APML) and 0.999984 (APML+MES). These figures measure **proxy geometry**, not classification generalization.

## Figure 7: repeatability on 3 paired seeds

[Open figure 7](../figures/07_mnist_c4_three_seed_regularity.svg).

Values taken from the robustness archive's `runs.csv`:

| Seed | APML min/mean distance | APML + MES min/mean distance |
|---|---:|---:|
| 42 | 0.782409 | 0.999984 |
| 43 | 0.977933 | 0.999970 |
| 44 | 0.968115 | 0.999983 |

The study also tracked within-class scatter, classification accuracy, tetrahedron volume quality and K-means NMI/ARI. The three-seed result supports **near-equidistance** of learned proxies under this particular setup. There are not enough seeds to infer universal behavior or statistical significance. Notably, classification improvements are **not uniform** over the paired seeds and should not be inferred from this figure.

## How this relates to the runnable repository

The repository's `scripts/reproduce.py mnist` runs a separate eight-epoch educational preset with **beta=0.2** and a disjoint display holdout selected exclusively from the official MNIST TRAIN split. Therefore **running the quick demo will not regenerate these exact 10-epoch/beta=1 archived figures**. Do not mislabel this separate demo as a replication of the archived quantitative results.

The numerical results in this document are derived from the provided reports. Publication of more complete archival raw data or exact historical training scripts would require a separate release and provenance review. The private research laboratory and its unpublished variants are outside the scope of this public-facing method repository.
